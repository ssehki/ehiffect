Your hair doesn’t end at the appointment.

Everything you need to keep your sew-in fresh and your natural hair healthy underneath.

First 48 hours

  • Hands off. No scratching, picking, or pulling at your braids while your scalp settles.
  • Some tenderness is normal. Sharp pain, bumps, or pulling that won’t ease up is not. Reach out to me.

Every night

  • Wrap your hair or wear a silk/satin bonnet.
  • A satin pillowcase is a bonus for less frizz and tangling.
  • Never go to bed with damp hair or damp braids.

Wash day

  • Cleanse your scalp every 1–2 weeks using diluted shampoo in an applicator bottle.
  • Rinse well. Leftover product causes buildup and itching.
  • Dry completely with a hooded dryer or blow dryer. Damp braids under an install can lead to mildew and odor.

Working out

  • Go for it! Pull your hair into a loose, low ponytail or use a sweatband. Nothing tight at the edges.
  • Sweat sitting in your braids causes itching and odor, so if you work out often, cleanse your scalp weekly.
  • After a sweaty session, blow-dry your roots on cool or low heat.

Be gentle

  • Detangle from ends to roots.
  • Use low to medium heat with a heat protectant. High heat shortens the life of your bundles.
  • Avoid tight styles and excessive tension.
  • Skip heavy products that weigh your hair down.

Your porosity

For your leave-out and your natural hair underneath.

Not sure? If your hair takes a while to get fully wet and products seem to sit on top, you’re likely low porosity. If it soaks up water fast and dries fast, you’re likely high. Or just ask me at your next appointment.

Low porosity

Moisture sits on top of your hair and products tend to build up.

  • Use light, water-based leave-ins and sprays.
  • Apply products to slightly damp or warm hair so they absorb. A warm towel helps.
  • Skip heavy butters and thick creams.
  • Less is more. Moisturize your leave-out every few days, not daily.

High porosity

Your hair soaks up moisture fast and loses it just as fast.

  • Moisturize, then seal: a leave-in first, then an oil or butter to lock it in.
  • Do a light protein treatment on your leave-out before your install and between installs.
  • Moisturize your leave-out daily or every other day.
  • In humid weather, use an anti-humidity product so your leave-out keeps blending.

Leave-out care

  • Keep it moisturized based on your porosity above.
  • Limit flat ironing so your leave-out doesn’t get heat damaged and stop blending.
  • Wrap or tuck it under your bonnet every night.

Sensitive scalp

  • Choose fragrance-free, alcohol-free scalp products.
  • Pure aloe vera gel or a gentle scalp spray calms itching without irritation.
  • Patch test anything new behind your ear first.
  • Be careful with tea tree and peppermint oils. They’re popular but can burn a sensitive scalp. Always dilute them, or skip them.
  • Rinse shampoo out extra well.
  • Let me know at your appointment so I can adjust braid tension for you.

6–8 weeks

  • It’s time for a takedown. Wearing your install longer risks matting, breakage, and buildup at the roots.
  • Book your takedown with me. Please don’t cut or pull your install out yourself.

Scalp feeling off?

Persistent pain, bumps, redness, worsening flakes, or irritation isn’t something to ignore. Reach out to me.
